Case study 01 · Chiropractic practice

9 in 10 claims denied. $350K+ recovered in 45 days.

The challenge

A chiropractic practice had almost stopped getting paid. About 90% of its claims were coming back denied, and the checks for the claims that were paid were being mailed to a wrong address, so even that money never reached the practice.

What I did
  • AR and denial audit: every open claim by payer and denial reason
  • Pay-to address corrected with every payer (demographic updates, W-9)
  • Misdirected checks traced, voided and reissued to the practice
  • EFT and ERA enrollment, so payments deposit directly and remits post electronically
  • Denied claims corrected, appealed and followed up to payment
  • Posting, reconciliation and a clean AR report for the owner

Result: more than $350,000 recovered in 45 days, with payments now arriving electronically instead of by mailed check.

Case study 02 · Out-of-network multi-specialty group

Underpaid and denied. $2.2M recovered through IDR, negotiation and AR appeals.

The challenge

An out-of-network group covering surgery, podiatry, orthopedics and pediatric/general care had a backlog of underpaid out-of-network claims sitting unresolved, plus rising denials and aged AR, and no view of what was recoverable.

What I did
  • Underpayment audit of the out-of-network claims, ranked by recoverable value
  • Dispute files built: claim, remittance, billed charges and clinical support
  • Out-of-network negotiations with payers before arbitration where possible
  • Independent Dispute Resolution (IDR) cases filed and tracked to the payment determination
  • Denied and aged AR recovered through appeals, reconsiderations, corrected claims and payer follow-up
  • Awards and payments posted; recoveries and remaining exposure reported to the group

Result: $2.2M recovered: higher payments on the disputed out-of-network claims through negotiation and IDR, and denied and aged AR collected through appeals and payer follow-up.
